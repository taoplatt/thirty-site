There's a natural pool (unheated), a tennis court, a lawn, and a games room with snooker and table tennis. Bedding and towels are provided.

What to bring

It'll be around 3–10°C.

  • Warm layers and a coat
  • Boots or wellies
  • Something with blue in it for Saturday
  • Running kit
  • Swimming things
  • Tennis racquet
  • A favourite game
  • Drinks and snacks to share
  • A camera

No need to bring bedding, towels or toiletries.

Please don't bring plastic confetti, balloons or dogs.
